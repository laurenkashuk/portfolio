Retrospekt 2020 – 2021
Twitch YouTube Instagram Mixed Reality Live Production Founder

A global virtual festival built from scratch — with broadcast-quality production and a mission people actually cared about.

When every live event on earth went dark during COVID, I invested my own money to produce Retrospekt: a global virtual music festival centered entirely on underrepresented voices — women, people of color, and the LGBTQIA+ community. This wasn't a livestream on a laptop. We designed immersive stages, brought in a full TV broadcast crew, ran one stage in mixed reality, and approached every set like a live television broadcast. Distributed across Twitch, YouTube, and Instagram simultaneously.

2.5MM unique viewers  ·  Apple Pay partnership  ·  13MM+ total social reach  ·  Fully self-funded

How I Think

Social creative is not a format.
It is a way of seeing.

Platform-native first

The idea has to belong to the platform. Not adapted for it. Born in it. I think in formats before I think in messages.

Culture before calendar

The best social moments are not planned six weeks out. I stay close enough to culture to move when something real is happening.

Breakthrough or nothing

Average creative is invisible. I push work until it has a genuine point of view — something people would share even if it wasn't an ad.

Data as a creative input

Numbers tell you what landed and why. I use performance data to get sharper, not safer. The goal is always better creative, not just better metrics.
